### Printing notes

- **Material:** PLA is fine for all of these.
- **Layer height:** 0.16–0.20 mm.
- **Infill:** 15–20 % is plenty — keep the parts light, they fly on a nano drone.
- **Supports:** generally not required; check the orientation in your slicer first.

Keep total added weight as low as possible — extra payload reduces flight time and stability.
